# 🚀 **Space Odyssey**

**Space Trail** is a Unity-based narrative resource-management game set in a dystopian future where Earth has become a wasteland. Inspired by the classic *Oregon Trail*, this game challenges you and your crew to escape the ruins of Earth by stealing a spaceship and surviving the perilous journey through space. Manage your fuel, tools, ship health, and money wisely—or risk death in the silent void.

## 📖 Story

The year is **39XX**. Earth has been devastated, stripped of all resources by centuries of exploitation. The rich have long since abandoned it for newer, better planets. But you? You didn’t have the luxury of wealth. So you did the only thing you could:

You stole a spaceship.

Now, along with your ragtag crew, you must navigate the stars in search of a better life—before the **Space Federation** catches you. Survive long enough, manage your dwindling resources, and maybe—just maybe—you’ll find a future worth living for.

> Serious at the start... but depending on how your crew interacts, things can get weirdly fun.

## 🔧 Core Mechanics

You must **balance survival and risk**. The following resources must be managed:

### ⛽ Fuel
- 🔥 **Depletes over time** as your ship moves forward.
- **If it hits zero: Game Over.**
- 🗨️ *"Made from ancient residues of long-dead plants and animals. Priority #1 unless you enjoy dying in deep space."*

### 🛠️ Tools
- 🔧 Used to **repair your ship** when damaged.
- **Integrity drops** the more stops you make.
- 🗨️ *"High-tech gear for high-stakes repairs. Not your first buy—but don’t forget it."*

### ❤️ Ship Health
- 💥 Damaged by certain **events or speeding too much**.
- **If it hits zero: Game Over.**
- 🗨️ *"Our flying tin can only takes so much. If it breaks, we’re space dust."*

### 💰 Money
- 💸 Used to **buy fuel, tools, and more** at space stations.
- 🗨️ *"Cash rules everything—even in space. Maybe consider 'finding' it in someone else's pocket..."*

---

## 🧠 Strategy Tips

- **Speed = Escape** but drains both fuel and ship health.
- **Stopping = Resources** but depletes your tool integrity.
- **Repairing ship = Costly**, think carefully before using it.
- **More stops = more risk**, especially since suits can’t be fixed.
- **Balance is key** — race too fast and you’ll burn out. Go too slow and the Space Federation will find you.

This project was created as a school assignment focused on systems design, resource balancing, and narrative branching. The goal was to reimagine a classic game structure (*Oregon Trail*) in a unique sci-fi setting.
